The problem

Most companies aren’t bad at hiring. They’re just too busy to do it well.

We hear the same four complaints on almost every first call. Here’s what we actually do about each one.

Ascend Technologies SaaS • 200-500 employees • VP Engineering

✕ THE PROBLEM

The role has been open for ten weeks. The job post is live everywhere, and the only applicants are people who are clearly applying to a hundred jobs at once.

✓ HOW AROHAAN SOLVED IT

We don’t wait for applicants. Most people we place weren’t job hunting when we called them, so you’re not fishing from the same shrinking pool as everyone else.

NovaHealth Healthcare • 1200-1500 employees

✕ THE PROBLEM

Your internal recruiter is covering fifteen open roles at the same time. Nobody’s getting real attention, and everyone knows it. Candidates are waiting too long for updates.

✓ HOW AROHAAN SOLVED IT

Each of our recruiters runs a handful of searches at once, not fifteen. That’s the whole reason a search moves in weeks instead of months.

GreenLeaf Foods Food & Beverages • 100-800 employees

✕ THE PROBLEM

The last hire looked great on paper, made it eight months, and now you’re paying to run the same search twice, plus the cost of the time he was ramping and the notice you had to give.

✓ HOW AROHAAN SOLVED IT

Every candidate is reference checked and skills tested before you see a resume, and permanent placements come with a 90-day replacement guarantee if it still doesn’t work out.

PulseLabs Healthcare • 1200-1500 employees

✕ THE PROBLEM

A staffing firm sends over thirty resumes for one role and maybe two of them are actually worth a first call. Now someone has to sort through the noise.

✓ HOW AROHAAN SOLVED IT

We’d rather send you three people you’d actually hire than thirty you have to screen yourself. If we don’t have a real fit yet, we say so instead of padding the list.

How it works

No black box, just four steps

You’ll get a status update every week a search is active. Not a form email, an actual update from the recruiter running it.

01

A real conversation

We ask about the role you actually need, which is usually different from the one in the job description. Sometimes we talk you out of requirements that would kill the search.

02

Outreach to people not looking

Most of the candidates we place weren’t browsing job boards. They were doing fine where they were until we called.

03

Vetting before you see anyone

References checked, skills tested, salary expectations confirmed. You get a short list, not a stack of maybes.

04

Staying on after the offer

We check in at 30, 60, and 90 days. If something’s off, we hear about it early enough to fix it.
