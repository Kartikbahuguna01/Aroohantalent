Four ways we work, depending on what you actually need

How we engage

Flexible models built around your hiring pace

Whether you need a confidential executive search or a dedicated recruitment squad, here is how we structure our work.

OUR SERVICES · 01 Executive Search
LEADERSHIP HIRING

The Right Leaders Change Everything.

  • C-suite, VP, and Director-level searches
  • Market mapping done before we approach anyone
  • We help you negotiate the offer, not just deliver a name
  • Usually 6 to 10 weeks, sometimes faster
OUR SERVICES · 02 Permanent Placement
FULL-TIME HIRING

Find Talent That Grows With You.

  • Sourcing, screening, and interview scheduling handled
  • Skills tests and reference checks included, not extra
  • The recruiter who starts the search finishes it
  • Usually 3 to 5 weeks
OUR SERVICES · 03 Contract Staffing
FLEXIBLE WORKFORCE

The Right Skills, Right on Time.

  • W-2 contractors, we run payroll
  • Convert to permanent whenever you’re ready, no penalty
  • Can usually place someone within a week
  • Engagements from a few weeks to a year
OUR SERVICES · 04 Recruitment Process
RECRUITMENT PARTNERSHIP

Scale Hiring Without the Complexity.

  • Recruiters who work inside your existing workflow
  • Scales with you, not a fixed headcount commitment
  • Reports straight out of your ATS, separate to check
  • Makes sense above roughly 20 hires a quarter
